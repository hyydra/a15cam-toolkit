## Findings

- **PRO** has manual `ISO`, `SPEED` (shutter), `EV`, `FOCUS`, `WB`. Tapping a header expands a slider (`iso_slider`, `shutter_speed_slider`, `focus_slider`) with a `manual_auto_button` toggle and an `activeTextView` that shows the current value. Defaults seen: ISO `A 5000` (auto, max 5000), SPEED `A 1/15`, FOCUS `AUTO`. Samsung's own pipeline is not bound by the Camera2 limits (Camera2 allows only 0.1 s and ignores focus).
- **NIGHT** shows an exposure timer (`time_indicator_text`, e.g. `2s`); the shutter is `Take picture`. Works end to end with `a15ui.py night`.
- **FUN** opens a consent dialog (`Continue`); intentionally not accepted by the script.
- Common controls: `Take picture`, `Switch to front camera`, `View pictures and videos`, `Quick controls`, zoom buttons `Zoom 0.5/1/2, Button`; top quick settings vary by mode (Flash, Resolution, Filters, Metering modes).
- `uiautomator dump` is flaky during animations; `a15ui.py tap` retries 3 times.
